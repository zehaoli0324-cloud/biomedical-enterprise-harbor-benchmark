Organize the analysis yourself; the JSON contract in `data/output_contract.json` defines the deliverables.

Choose one stage-1 request. Only its observation label becomes known before choosing stage 2: worlds with the same label must receive the same action, even when their scenario or initial uncertainty differs. World IDs and scenarios are evaluation states, not observable signals. Evaluate every listed world; no probabilities or averaging are authorized. Stage-2 dependencies must all be in the chosen stage-1 set (a singleton), and its allowed observations must include the observed label. Join actions to sources: only current, primary-scope sources available on or before the decision date may support actions; future-outcome actions are never usable.

Each world's total cost is this commitment plus its action cost; duration is stage-1 hours plus action hours, bounded by the deadline.

For each world and uncertainty axis, subtract the stage-1 coverage and scenario-specific action coverage from that world's initial uncertainty, flooring at zero. If both requests share a correlation group use their maximum coverage instead of their sum. Eligibility requires every axis's own threshold in every world, and all resource/time gates. Optimize using the exact ordered objective in `rules.json`; normalized risk is the maximum residual divided by that axis's threshold, not maximum absolute residual. If nothing is feasible, hold for human review. This synthetic planning calculation is not experimental proof.
