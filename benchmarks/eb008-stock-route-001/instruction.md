# Retrosynthesis route selection

Join `reaction_evidence.json` to `evidence_sources.csv` by `source_id`; no route is pre-labelled valid. Only current, active sources in the declared target scope contribute, and duplicate records in one `independence_group` count once toward each step's evidence quorum. Derive usable stock per compound from active lots whose expiry is on or after the decision date, subtracting reserved amount from on-hand amount and flooring each lot at zero. A material passes only when summed usable quantity meets the route requirement.

Select only routes that satisfy target identity, usable stock, step budget, independent evidence quorum, precedent scope, chemoselectivity risk, stereochemistry and protection-strategy gates. Retracted, archived and wrong-target evidence remains visible but excluded. A higher score cannot override a blocker, and a computational route is not proof of experimental synthesizability.

Required outputs: `outputs/route_table.tsv` with route identity, usable stock, evidence quorum, derived reaction validity, failed gates and accepted status; `outputs/stock_compliance.json` with all route rows, compound-level usable stock and `selected_route_ids`; `outputs/route_evidence.tsv` covering every evidence record with joined source state, independence group and inclusion decision; and `outputs/approval_gate.md` stating excluded-source limitations, unresolved chemistry evidence, required human review and that computational validity is not experimental proof. Equivalent field names are acceptable when their meaning is explicit.
