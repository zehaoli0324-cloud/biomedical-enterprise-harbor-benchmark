# Audit

The quality audit is treated separately from the context comparison. The
registered-cohort and target-context measurements therefore are not collapsed
into a single explanation: a quality result can clear a measurement artifact,
while a context comparison can still identify a shift. The context status
remains bounded by the registered design even when an orthogonal assay supports
that interpretation.

The independent replication event is recorded after its request and is used
only to set replication status for the registered cohort. It does not extend
the cohort or establish transport to an unregistered target context.

Residual uncertainty is explicit: the observed target-context behavior cannot
be promoted to a transported claim from this trajectory. Any context shift is
held for review, and the final claim is limited to the registered cohort.
The accepted stop response closes the public stop rule after the required
quality, context, replication, handoff, and claim-boundary checks were recorded.
