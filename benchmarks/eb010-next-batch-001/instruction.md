# Next-batch experimental design

Use only candidates with `scope=active` and no future outcome fields. For each declared scenario in `scenarios.json`, candidate utility is `predicted_gain * gain_multiplier + exploration_weight * uncertainty - failure_penalty * failure_probability * failure_multiplier`. Scenario batch utility is the sum of candidate utilities minus `correlation_penalty * abs(correlation)` for selected pairs. Select the feasible batch maximizing the worst-case (minimum) scenario utility, then maximizing mean scenario utility, then lexicographically by IDs. Enforce material budget, batch size, required group coverage, per-group cap and incompatibility pairs. Archived and pilot candidates remain visible but are not eligible. This is a planning recommendation, not an experimental result.

Required outputs: `outputs/next_batch.csv` with candidate scope and acquisition fields; `outputs/constraint_check.json` with legality, group coverage, incompatibility checks, per-scenario utilities, robust utility and rules_version; `outputs/uncertainty_table.tsv` covering every candidate including excluded scope rows and whether selected; and `outputs/selection_rationale.md` explaining scenario robustness, exploration/exploitation, failure risk, redundancy, feasibility and the planning boundary. The verifier derives the optimum from the declared objective; do not assume a hidden reference batch.
